Sharpen this offer: **$ARGUMENTS**

Work through:

1. **Who exactly** — not a demographic. The person at a specific moment, with a specific problem, who has just realized they need something. If the answer is "anyone who…", the offer is too diffuse to sell.

2. **The problem in their words.** How they'd describe it to a friend — not how the business describes it. The gap between those two is usually why an offer doesn't land.

3. **What they actually get** — deliverables, access, duration, format. Concrete enough to picture. Vagueness here reads as evasion, and buyers price it as risk.

4. **The transformation** — where they are before, where they are after. One line each.

5. **Why now** — what makes this urgent for them. Not manufactured scarcity; the real cost of waiting another quarter.

6. **The objection that kills it.** Every offer has one thing the buyer thinks right before they don't buy. Name it and answer it directly.

7. **What it isn't.** Naming who this is wrong for makes it credible for everyone else.

Constraints:

- **Never invent a price.** If pricing is relevant and I haven't given you one, flag it as needed — don't fill it in.
- Don't promise outcomes that depend on the buyer's own effort without saying so.
- If this repo has `gss-review-loop/standards/current-state.md`, check any price, tier, or claim against it before using it.
