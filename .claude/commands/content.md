Generate content angles on: **$ARGUMENTS**

Give me **8 angles**, each genuinely different in argument — not eight ways of saying the same thing with different opening words. If two angles would produce the same piece, one of them is filler; replace it.

For each:

- **The angle** — one line, stated as a position, not a topic. "Most people sequence the visa before the tax residency, and it costs them" is an angle. "Visa considerations" is a topic.
- **Who it's for** — the specific reader who stops scrolling at this one.
- **The hook** — the actual opening line, written out. Not a description of a hook.
- **Why it works** — one clause. Tension, contrarian take, named mistake, unexpected number.

Spread across these types so the set doesn't collapse into one register:

- The **mistake** everyone makes
- The **contrarian** take on accepted advice
- The **specific number** that reframes the problem
- The **story** — one person, one decision, one consequence
- The **comparison** — this vs. that, and when each wins
- The **process** revealed — what actually happens behind the scenes
- The **question** the audience is embarrassed to ask
- The **cost of doing nothing**

Then: **rank the top 3** and say which single one you'd write first, and why.

If I named a platform, match its shape and length. If not, write them platform-neutral and note which suits which.
