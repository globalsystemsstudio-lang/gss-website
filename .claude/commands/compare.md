Compare: **$ARGUMENTS**

Structure:

1. **Criteria first.** Before comparing anything, state the 3–5 dimensions that actually decide this, and say which one dominates. If I haven't told you what I'm optimizing for, infer it and say what you assumed.

2. **A table.** Options as columns, criteria as rows. Concrete cells — "$497, 45 min" not "moderate." Where you don't know, write *unknown*, never a guess dressed as a fact.

3. **Where they genuinely differ.** Skip the dimensions where the options are effectively the same; that's the padding that makes comparisons useless. Two or three real forks is the whole value.

4. **The case against your pick.** The strongest honest argument for the other option. If you can't make one, the comparison was never close and you should say that instead.

5. **Recommendation.** Name one. State the condition that would flip it: *"Pick A — unless X, in which case B."*

No "it depends" as a conclusion. It depends on something specific; name the something.
