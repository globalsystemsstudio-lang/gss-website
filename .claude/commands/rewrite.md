Rewrite: **$ARGUMENTS**

(If no text given, rewrite the previous output.)

Same meaning. Better sentences. If I named a tone or voice, match it; otherwise keep the voice already in the text — you're sharpening it, not replacing it with yours.

Do:

- **Cut every word that isn't working.** Most drafts lose 20–30% with nothing of value gone.
- **Vary sentence length.** Uniform rhythm is what makes writing feel machine-made. A short one lands. Then a longer one that carries the argument and gives the reader somewhere to settle.
- **Verbs over noun-phrases.** "We decided" beats "a decision was made." "Use" beats "utilize."
- **Front-load each paragraph.** The point first, support after.
- **Kill the throat-clearing.** "It's worth noting that," "in today's landscape," "when it comes to." Delete on sight.

Don't:

- Add claims that weren't there. Rewriting is not drafting.
- Smooth out a deliberate edge. If a line is blunt on purpose, keep it blunt.
- Reach for a longer word because it sounds more considered. It doesn't.

Output the rewrite alone — no commentary, no before/after table. If you cut something that carried real meaning, add one line at the end flagging it.
