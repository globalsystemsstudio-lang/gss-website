description: Make it simple and genuinely easy to understand
argument-hint: [concept] — optionally: for [audience]
---

Explain: **$ARGUMENTS**

If I named an audience, write for exactly them. If not, assume a smart person with zero background in this specific area — not a child, not a specialist.

How:

1. **One plain sentence first.** What it is, no jargon at all. If you can't manage it in one sentence, you don't understand it well enough yet.
2. **Why it matters** — the thing that goes wrong without it. Concrete, not abstract.
3. **How it works** — the mechanism, in the order someone would actually encounter it. Numbered if there are steps.
4. **One real example**, with real numbers or names. A worked example beats three paragraphs of description.
5. **The part people get wrong.** Every concept has one. Name it.

Constraints:

- Every term of art gets defined on first use, in-line, in five words or fewer.
- No analogies that need their own explanation. A bad analogy costs more than plain description.
- Short sentences. Vary the length so it reads like speech, not documentation.
- Don't flatter the question or announce what you're about to do. Just explain.
