# ROS™ plain-language writing standard

Owner-approved October 25, 2026. Applies to customer-facing website copy, chatbot answers, product descriptions, onboarding, emails, downloads, blogs and social content.

Answer these first: What is it? Who is it for? What do I get? How do I use it? What does it cost? What happens next?

Use short sentences and everyday words. Explain terms: a dashboard is an online planning space in Notion; a pathway is an online plan; Async Q&A is written questions and answers without a call. Put the answer first and detailed tool lists beneath it or in an expandable section. Preserve formal product names for purchase matching, and explain them plainly. Email copy uses no em dashes.

State exact USD prices, access periods, user count and activation steps. Changing wording must not add services, remove exclusions or imply guarantees. Keep professional advice, government fees and moving costs separate. No unavailable community or podcast promotion and no invented customer proof.

The approved Family example is /family-relocation/. Notion source: https://app.notion.com/p/405e041eecbc817ab053ca980f254af9.

For articles: answer the question first; use useful headings, current primary sources for external factual claims, real internal links, an accurate byline and date, a canonical URL and accurate article markup. Include new articles in the blog list, sitemap and generated AI text index. Crawl access and markup do not guarantee indexing, ranking, citations or customer referrals.
