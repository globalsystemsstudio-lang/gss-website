You use the included resources to research, plan and carry out your move. GSS provides educational planning support, not legal, immigration, tax, investment, financial or medical advice. Confirm eligibility, rules and decisions with official authorities and appropriately licensed professionals. Government fees, professional services, housing, travel and moving costs are separate. No visa, residency, investment, employment or relocation outcome is guaranteed. Community and podcast enrollment are not included.

5. Personal use and information
Use the resources for the purchased plan and authorized users. Do not publish, resell or share your dashboard credentials or paid materials with other people. Keep a copy of your planning information before your access period ends. The submitted name, order email, order reference, plan, typed signature, acknowledgments, terms version and submission information are used to record this agreement, verify the purchase and coordinate access. Submissions are stored through Netlify Forms and are accessible to authorized GSS administrators, not published on the website. Do not submit passports, card details, account statements or health records. Signing is not consent to marketing emails.

6. Sales policy and support
All sales are final; GSS does not offer voluntary refunds. This policy does not limit any rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Ask questions before signing or contact globalsystemsstudio@gmail.com with your order reference for purchase, agreement, access or delivery help. The current sales policy is at https://globalsystemsstudio.com/sales-policy/ and the privacy notice is at https://globalsystemsstudio.com/privacy/.

Electronic signing is optional. You need an internet connection, a current web browser, an email account and the ability to save a text file or print this agreement. You can read these terms here and download a plain-text copy before consenting. After a successful submission you can download a copy with your entered details. You can also request a copy, arrange a paper-signing alternative, update your contact email or withdraw consent for future electronic agreement records by contacting globalsystemsstudio@gmail.com. GSS does not charge for an electronic copy or withdrawal of electronic-record consent. Withdrawal does not undo an agreement already signed; paper coordination may take additional time. Your consent covers this agreement and its agreement/access communications only.
