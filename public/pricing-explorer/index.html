Before you choose

How does access begin?

Complete your PayPal purchase and sign the agreement. GSS then follows up to arrange your Notion planning space. Your access period begins after completed payment and a signed agreement. Setup is coordinated by GSS.

Is this a subscription?

No. Each pathway is a one-time purchase for its stated initial period. Renewal is optional. Renewal prices are shown in plan details; confirm the renewal length and terms with GSS before paying.

What does the price cover?

The listed planning resources, support and access period. You carry out your move. Government fees, licensed professional services and moving expenses are separate. ROS™ offers education and planning support; confirm legal, tax, immigration, investment and medical decisions with qualified professionals.

What should I review before buying?

Review the plan’s full inclusion list and the sales terms. All sales are final, with no refunds. Read the full sales policy →
